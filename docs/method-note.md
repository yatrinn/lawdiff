# LawDiff — Method note

**RealPage Rental Housing Law Navigator · Hack-Nation 7 · Yannik Trinn, solo**
Repository: https://github.com/yatrinn/lawdiff · Demo: https://yatrinn.github.io/lawdiff/

**Scope.** A research prototype for the supplied 500-address sample in California, New Jersey and Massachusetts, as of 2026-10-01. A legal change leads to address-level review, captured passages and missing evidence. This is not legal advice or a compliance certification.

**Sources and extraction.** Recorded, tool-free Codex CLI calls extract candidates from captured text. AI-assisted source review is not independent legal review. A hash-pinned manifest selects 133 unchanged records from 24 sources. Archived outputs and per-rule hashes preserve their origin. This selected pack drives both browser and submission results. Santa Ana contributes 26 records, 26 with narrative coverage. Corpus receipts retain failed and unprocessed sources; processing does not establish complete interpretation.

**Execution.** One shared engine uses true, false and unknown, official legal-city evidence, operative dates and exclusive end dates. A decisive false condition can exclude a rule despite missing facts. 22 records have executable conditions; 111 retain coverage prose and cannot establish address applicability until interpretation review. Explicit assessor apartment classes can establish residential use, never primary residence or absent exemptions. Pending proposals stay inactive. Simulations are excluded from original-sample exports.

**Validation.** Build gates reconcile catalog, candidate, selection and original-run hashes, exact quotations, dates and source identities. Regression tests challenge tampering, malformed conditions and jurisdiction/date errors. Exact quotes do not prove interpretation. The supplied participant v5 package contains no score.py or answer key; no official accuracy score is claimed.

**Changes.** Qualitative checks satisfy the supplied expectations for T1 and T4. T2: partial; T3: partial; T5: partial. T2 has 91 unresolved addresses; T3 has 140 after its operative date and 0 established conflict flags. T5 reports 0 affected addresses, with the failed-status record still missing. These checks are not jury scores. No missing exemption or occupancy fact is invented to match an expected result.

**Build snapshot, October 4, 2026.** 133 selected records; 133 exact quotation spans; 110 source IDs, 73 texts; 28 sources processed, 37 text-missing, 38 rejected/withheld, 7 unprocessed; 491/500 legal-city matches. Counts are not accuracy percentages. Reproduce using the recorded-run archive and documented selection.

**Limits and next step.** Source coverage, ownership/occupancy facts, legal interactions and interpretation remain incomplete. Census evidence uses interpolation or strict jurisdiction consensus, not parcel certification; ambiguous coordinates stay unset. Historical scenarios reuse supplied facts. Spanish summaries are unreviewed machine translations. Expert review and a narrow paid pilot must establish accuracy, review effort and buyer demand. No customer, measured savings or validated willingness to pay is claimed.
