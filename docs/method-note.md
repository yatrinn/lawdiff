# LawDiff — Method note

**RealPage Rental Housing Law Navigator · Hack-Nation 7 · Yannik Trinn, solo**
Repository: https://github.com/yatrinn/lawdiff · Demo: https://yatrinn.github.io/lawdiff/

**Scope.** A research prototype for the supplied 500-address sample in California, New Jersey and Massachusetts, as of 2026-10-01. A change leads to address-level review, original passages and missing evidence. This is not legal advice or a compliance certification.

**Sources and extraction.** Recorded, tool-free Codex CLI calls extract candidates from captured text. Source review is AI-assisted, not independent legal review. An explicit, hash-pinned manifest selects 89 unchanged records from 17 sources. The original outputs, review notes and hashes are archived. This same selected pack drives browser and submission results. Santa Ana contributes 26 narrative records, including the separately captured 2026 status resolution. The complete corpus receipt includes failed and unprocessed sources; no full-corpus success is claimed.

**Execution.** One shared engine uses true, false and unknown, verified jurisdiction, operative dates and exclusive end dates. A decisive false condition can exclude a rule despite missing facts. Thirteen records have executable conditions; 76 retain coverage prose and cannot establish address applicability until specialist interpretation review. Pending proposals never become active by moving time forward. Browser simulations are excluded from sample exports and shared original-data rights cards.

**Validation.** Build gates reconcile catalog, candidate, selection and original-run hashes, exact quotations, dates and source identities. Regression tests challenge tampered sources, malformed conditions and jurisdiction/date errors. Exact quotation matches do not prove interpretation. The supplied participant v5 package contains no score.py or answer key; no official accuracy score is claimed.

**Changes.** The actual qualitative report passes T1 and T4. T2 retains 93 unresolved addresses; T3 retains 140 after its operative date and lacks established state/local conflicts. T5 produces no active cap, but no automatic failed-status record was accepted. These are disclosed partial cases, not passing scores. The report uses original sample facts without invented exemptions or property use.

**Build snapshot, October 4, 2026.** 89 selected records; 89 exact primary quotations; 110 source IDs, 73 texts; 21 sources processed, 37 text-missing, 44 rejected/withheld, 8 unprocessed; 475/500 legal-city matches. Counts are not accuracy percentages. Reproduce using the recorded-run archive and documented selection.

**Limits and next step.** Source coverage, property-use/owner/occupancy facts, legal interactions and interpretation remain incomplete. Census matches are interpolation, not parcel certification; historical scenarios reuse the supplied facts. Spanish summaries are unreviewed machine translations. Expert review and a narrowly scoped paid pilot must establish accuracy, review effort and buyer demand before production use. No customer, measured savings or validated willingness to pay is claimed.
