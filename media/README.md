# LawDiff video assets

- `lawdiff-demo.mp4`: 56-second narrated walkthrough assembled from actual interface captures. [Watch online](https://yatrinn.github.io/lawdiff/media/lawdiff-demo.mp4).
- `lawdiff-tech.mp4`: 56-second narrated technical explanation with authored architecture diagrams. [Watch online](https://yatrinn.github.io/lawdiff/media/lawdiff-tech.mp4).
- Both final films are 1920 × 1080, H.264, 30 fps, with AAC audio and burned-in English captions. They visibly disclose synthetic narration.
- English narration uses the **Leslie preset synthetic voice**, generated through Runway with Eleven Multilingual v2. It is not a cloned voice or Yannik's voice.
- `narration/demo.json` and `narration/tech.json` contain the actual spoken text, chapter timing and hashes of the recorded audio inputs. The accompanying `lawdiff-*.srt` files follow that narration; `lawdiff-*.probe.json` records the final encoded media properties.
- `VIDEO_NOTES.md` and `TECH_VIDEO_NOTES.md` document the picture edit, narration and reproduction steps.

Files named `*-DRAFT.srt` are historical preparation files, not the final demo/tech transcripts. Use `lawdiff-demo.srt` and `lawdiff-tech.srt` with the finished films. Neither film presents a continuous screen recording or a live model call.

The participant's authentic team video and photograph remain to be recorded. Both event submissions still require the participant's action; a published film is not a submission confirmation.
